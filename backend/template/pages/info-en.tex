There are a total of \pageCount\ pages. There are \schmierblaetteranzahl\ blank pages at the end of the exam for your personal notes. You may use these pages for your answers if the provided solution space is not enough. Please write down a page reference under the assignment and note in your solution to which assignment number you are referring. If we cannot match your solution to an assignment, it will not be graded.

There are some single and multiple response questions for which you need to mark the correct statements. If you mark a wrong statement you will get 0 points for your answer, even if you additionally marked the correct statement. Please mark your answer with a cross. Should you need to cancel your answer, cancel the whole column of boxes \underline{clearly} and draw a new column of boxes.

\ifthenelse{\equal{\englishandgerman}{yes}}{
  The exam is provided in German and an English translation for your convenience. Please use either the English \underline{or} the German one for writing your solution! Remember, that the German version is the legally binding one! You may give your answers in German or English.

For the exam no teaching aids are allowed (\underline{closed book exam}). Any kind of portable communication device is forbidden. You may, however, use a language dictionary book. The duration is \examduration\ minutes.

Answers in unreadable handwriting cannot be graded. Please use permanent ink pens (no pencils). At the end of the exam, leave all paper on the desk, no matter whether the paper has been written on or not. Do not remove any material from the room. Deceits and disturbances are treated accordingly to the examination regulations (Prüfungsordnung).
